WHAT CONSENSUS SAYS

Water does NOT fall off — the ice wall contains it. The edge waterfall is an outsider meme; flat earthers disavow it. Gravity is replaced by density & buoyancy + Universal Acceleration (9.8 m/s² upward). The moon is self-luminous cold light. Eclipses are caused by the Shadow Object. Southern 24h daylight is denied by the community.
